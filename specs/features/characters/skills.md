# Skills

A Skill is the ability for a character to perform a specific action.
Each skill is associated with a characteristic.

### Dodge

### Stealth

(Silent Movement, Camouflage, Disguise, Theft)
Linked to all actions one wishes to hide from others, such as moving silently, hiding in the shadows, or stealing an
object.

## Constitution

### Vigor

Ability to perform actions despite physical injuries.

### Endurance

Resistance to intense or long activities, involving loss of energy.

### Recovery

Ability to physically recover from damage sustained in combat or from a difficult physical task (speed of physical
recovery or recovery from being stunned, for example).

### Immunity

Resistance to poisons, drugs, narcotics, and all kinds of viruses.

## Cunning

### Piloting

(Tau'ri, Goa'uld, Ancient, Other)

### Sabotage

Destroy or trap an object. Picking locks on doors or computer hacking depend on the Sabotage skill. Trapping an area
with mines, for example, is also part of this skill.

### Military Strategy

Understanding how the enemy will react and how to counter them. Successfully setting up an ambush depends on a Military
Strategy roll.

### Survival

Set of techniques used to get by in a hostile environment: tracking, foraging for food, building shelter, etc.

## Charisma

All forms of torture, and persuasion attempts involving a minimum of violence, depend on Intimidation.

### Leadership

Ability to lead troops or populations to follow precise orders. Military leaders have excellent Leadership. Also used to
determine a person's rank.

## Using Skills

Nearly all tests follow the same rule.
Roll 2D10, add the value of the associated skill, and compare the result to the difficulty level associated with the
action.

| Difficulty Level | Associated Difficulty Threshold |
|:-----------------|:-------------------------------:|
| Easy             |                5                |
| Common           |               10                |
| Difficult        |               15                |
| Very Difficult   |               20                |
| Superhuman       |              25 +               |

Difficulty Threshold can take into account favorable or unfavorable factors by decreasing or increasing the threshold by 2.
